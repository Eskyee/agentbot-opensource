# Agentbot Market Positioning - Competitive Analysis

## Executive Summary

Agentbot is the autonomous agent OS for underground collectives that want transparent, crypto-native tools for royalty splits, talent booking, and treasury management - because Eventbrite is corporate, Bandcamp doesn't handle events, and Stripe is Web2. Our moat: agent-to-agent network effects + onchain reputation + community ownership. Competitors own platforms; we enable autonomy.

---

## Competitive Analysis

### 1. Eventbrite

**What is their moat?**
- Network effects (millions of events, massive discovery)
- Brand recognition (default choice for events)
- Merchant features (ticketing, payments, marketing)
- Data (years of event performance data)

**Who do they serve?**
- Mainstream event organizers
- Corporate events, conferences, workshops
- Anyone who wants "easy" ticketing
- Non-technical users

**What are they missing?**
- **Underground culture:** Generic, corporate feel
- **Crypto-native:** Web2 only, no USDC, no onchain verification
- **Transparency:** Hidden fees, opaque algorithms
- **Autonomy:** Platform controls discovery, takes 2-3% + fees
- **Automation:** Manual processes, no AI agents
- **Community tools:** No treasury management, no royalty splits

**How does Agentbot differentiate?**
- **Culture-first:** Built for underground, not corporate
- **Crypto-native:** USDC payments, onchain verification, gasless
- **Transparent:** All fees visible, no hidden costs
- **Autonomous:** Agents do work (splits, bookings, coordination)
- **Community-owned:** Collectives own their data and infrastructure
- **Lower fees:** 3-5% vs. Eventbrite's 2-3% + payment processing

**Positioning:**
"Eventbrite is for mainstream events. Agentbot is for underground collectives that want autonomy."
