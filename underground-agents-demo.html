Real Use Cases

🎪 Underground Collective

A crew of 12 people runs monthly warehouse parties. They use the Rave Event Agent to manage guest lists (avoiding WhatsApp chaos), the Treasury Agent to track shared funds transparently, and the Content Agent to generate flyers and social posts. All payments in USDC, no bank accounts needed.

🔊 Soundsystem Crew

A soundsystem collective owns £15K of equipment. They use the Soundsystem Agent to track inventory, manage bookings, and generate quotes. The Treasury Agent handles deposits and reimbursements. Everything onchain, fully transparent.

🌍 International Collective

Members across 5 countries coordinate events. The Treasury Agent enables borderless payments in USDC. The Event Agent coordinates across time zones. No banks, no borders, no bullshit.
