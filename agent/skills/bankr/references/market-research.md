# Market Research

AI-powered token analysis across multiple blockchains.

## Capabilities

- Current pricing and 24h/7d/30d price changes
- Market cap, trading volume, supply metrics
- Technical indicators: RSI, MACD
- Trending tokens and top gainers/losers
- Sentiment analysis

## Supported Chains

Base, Polygon, Ethereum, Solana, Unichain.

## Prompt Examples

```
"What tokens are trending on Base?"
"Analyze ETH price"
"What are the top gainers today?"
"Compare USDC vs DAI"
"What's the RSI on BTC?"
"Show me the market cap of BNKR"
```

## Important

Analysis is informational only, not investment advice. Cross-reference multiple metrics and perform independent research before trading. Exercise caution with newly launched tokens and low-volume assets.
